# Domino vs firewall

## Domino (prion)

- **Self-template:** PrPSc converts PrPC without external enzyme.
- **Irreversible** at organism scale once load is high.
- **Species barrier:** sequence mismatch slows the domino (S5-A-R2).

## Firewall (ecology)

- **Cannibalism taboo** in most social species — behavioral.
- **Disease cost** when violated — kuru, BSE, CWD (S6-B-R1).
- **No firewall for war** — same species, no digestion, no R₀ collapse (S7-A).

## «Fix cannibalism» (S5-A-R3)

Even if PrP lowering removed prion fuel, **viruses, bacteria, kuru-like selection** remain. Science cannot «approve» conspecific consumption — only map residual risk.
