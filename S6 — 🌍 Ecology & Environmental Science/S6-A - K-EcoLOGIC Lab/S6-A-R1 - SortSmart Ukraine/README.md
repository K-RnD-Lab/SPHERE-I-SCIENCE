# S6-A-R1 - SortSmart Ukraine

`S6-A-R1` is the flagship research program under `K-EcoLOGIC Lab`.

Its purpose is to turn fragmented Ukrainian waste and environmental data into a usable analytical system for:

- sorting-readiness analysis
- recovery-gap estimation
- circular-material prioritization
- climate-impact modeling

## Live Surface

- public platform
  - `https://k-ecologic-lab.streamlit.app/`
- flagship page
  - `SortSmart Ukraine`

## Why this belongs in S6

The research unit is an environmental system:

- waste generation
- landfill dependence
- regional infrastructure
- pollution-linked environmental effects

That makes it a natural fit for `S6 - Ecology & Environmental Science`.

`R1c` adds a verified Kyiv baseline, corrects unsupported waste claims, separates
EU recycling from energy-recovery categories, and defines the policy and
investment conditions required before selecting a processing plant.
