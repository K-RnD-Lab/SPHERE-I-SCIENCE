## Core question

Can long-run human population, birth, and death data reveal repeatable turnover patterns, and how should a "rebirth" or "pererodzhennia" idea be handled without treating metaphysical claims as statistical facts?

## Boundary rule

This study does not test reincarnation as a literal spiritual mechanism.

It can test demographic turnover patterns:

- how many people are born and die over time
- whether births and deaths show cycles, shocks, or transitions
- how population replacement changes under mortality decline, fertility decline, war, disease, and modernization
- whether symbolic "rebirth" language maps to measurable population renewal

## Evidence limits

| Period | What can be analyzed | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 CE to 1800 | reconstructed population milestones | not direct annual birth/death records |
| 1800 to 1950 | improving historical population estimates and selected national vital data | incomplete global birth/death coverage |
| 1950 to present | UN WPP births, deaths, fertility, mortality, life expectancy | estimates still vary by data quality |

## Working hypotheses

### H1: Demographic turnover is not cyclical in a simple mystical rhythm.

Births and deaths are shaped by mortality decline, fertility transition, disease, war, migration, and economic systems.

### H2: "Rebirth" can be reframed as population renewal.

The measurable version is:

```text
annual births and deaths create a continuous replacement flow, but not evidence of identity transfer.
```

### H3: Population pressure and pollution pressure can be compared, but not collapsed.

Population growth, energy systems, industry, agriculture, and consumption intensity interact. Population alone does not explain pollution.

## Claim discipline

Allowed:

- "This chart shows demographic turnover."
- "The rebirth idea can be translated into a measurable renewal metaphor."
- "The data do not support identity-level claims."

Not allowed:

- "Statistics establish identity-level rebirth claims."
- "Births and deaths are synchronized by a spiritual mechanism."
- "Aggregate demography can detect individual reincarnation."
