## Excluded pending evidence

- a causal link between Chornobyl and the commissioning of Landfill No. 5;
- exact pre-1986 Kyiv disposal sites and tonnages;
- unsupported claims that 70-80% of Soviet-era waste was organic or paper;
- claims that Europe recycles nearly all municipal waste;
- current Kyiv treatment shares inferred from historical municipal statements.

## Update rule

Prefer, in order: live administrative/open data, audited operator records, official monitoring, primary legal text, official planning documents, peer-reviewed research, and clearly labeled investigative reporting. Never overwrite a historical value with a newer value; append a new timepoint.
