## Core question

How have pollution pressures changed globally and in Ukraine across air, water, and land, and which indicators can be visualized first without overclaiming coverage?

## Why this belongs in S6

This study is ecology- and environment-facing. It is not only a civic dashboard; it asks how pollution pressure can be represented as a system of environmental stress signals.

## Current interpretation

Renewable energy reduces future fossil-fuel emissions when it replaces high-carbon energy, but it does not automatically remove accumulated atmospheric pollution or repair water and land damage.

That means this study should separate:

- emission prevention
- pollutant removal
- ecosystem recovery
- monitoring and accountability
- biochemical or chemical remediation hypotheses

## Ukraine-specific research lenses

- industrial and urban air exposure
- war-related environmental damage
- water-system disruption and monitoring gaps
- soil contamination, mining, erosion, and agricultural pressure
- permits, polluters, and public oversight

## Visual-first plan

1. Global CO2 and Ukraine CO2 timeline.
2. PM2.5 exposure timeline for world and Ukraine.
3. Water access and wastewater treatment indicators.
4. Land and soil pressure panel: pesticides, fertilizer, land degradation, soil organic carbon.
5. Cross-pressure dashboard: air + water + land + population pressure.

## Claim discipline

Allowed:

- "This indicator suggests pressure or exposure context."
- "This trend is visible in the selected source."
- "Coverage is incomplete and must be shown."

Not allowed:

- "Alternative energy alone solves current atmospheric pollution."
- "A single indicator proves total environmental damage."
- "Ukraine-wide pollution is fully represented by one international dataset."
- "This is a health diagnosis or emergency-warning system."
