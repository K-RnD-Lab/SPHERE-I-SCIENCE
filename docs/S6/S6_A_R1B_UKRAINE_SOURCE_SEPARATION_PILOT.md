# S6-A-R1b Ukraine Source-Separation Pilot

## Purpose

Design a small, measurable Ukrainian source-separation pilot that verifies the full route from household to processor. Adding colored bins without a collection and processing contract is not counted as recycling.

## Legal and operating basis

Ukraine's Law `On Waste Management` establishes this priority order:

```text
prevention -> preparation for reuse -> recycling -> recovery -> disposal
```

The law provides for separate collection of paper/cardboard, plastic, glass, and metal, with conditions for biowaste, textiles, hazardous household waste, and other streams. Local government is responsible for organizing municipal waste management and separate collection.

## Pilot boundary

- scale: one apartment building, institution, school, office, or compact neighborhood;
- duration: 8 to 12 weeks after a two-week baseline audit;
- participation: opt-in with clear instructions and no personal waste profiling;
- prerequisite: written confirmation from collectors/processors for every accepted stream;
- excluded: medical, chemical, explosive, asbestos, radioactive, and unknown hazardous waste.

## Stream design

The exact bins must follow local processor requirements. A practical starting configuration is:

1. accepted dry recyclables, separated further where the processor requires it;
2. clean glass where a verified glass route exists;
3. organics only where composting or anaerobic-digestion capacity is contracted;
4. residual waste;
5. supervised drop-off for batteries, lamps, e-waste, medicines, and other hazardous household streams through authorized routes.

If no verified destination exists, do not claim diversion. Record the infrastructure gap instead.

## Measurement protocol

### Baseline

- total waste mass per week;
- composition audit from a safe representative sample;
- current collection cost and frequency;
- documented destination and landfill route;
- resident knowledge and willingness survey.

### Pilot metrics

- `capture rate = correctly separated target material / estimated target material generated`;
- `contamination rate = wrong material in a separated stream / separated stream mass`;
- `verified diversion rate = mass accepted by a verified recovery route / total waste mass`;
- kilograms per participant and week;
- participation and bin-overflow rate;
- collection and processing cost per tonne;
- rejected-load rate and reason;
- estimated CO2e benefit shown separately from measured mass;
- final destination evidence: ticket, processor receipt, or auditable transfer record.

## Minimum experiment design

| Phase | Duration | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| baseline | 2 weeks | measure current mixed waste and route |
| setup | 1 week | confirm processors, labels, bins, safety, and collection schedule |
| pilot A | 4 weeks | launch simple separation and weekly measurement |
| improvement | 1 week | revise labels/placement from contamination evidence |
| pilot B | 4 weeks | repeat and compare with pilot A |
| closeout | 1 week | verify destinations, costs, lessons, and scale/no-scale decision |

## Success gates

- every stream has a confirmed downstream route;
- contamination remains below the processor's rejection threshold;
- verified diversion improves over baseline;
- no unsafe manual sorting or hazardous exposure occurs;
- cost and collection workload are reported honestly;
- results are reproducible and do not rely only on participant self-report.

## Research questions

- Which stream design gives the highest verified diversion with the lowest contamination?
- Do labels, bin placement, feedback, or incentives change sorting behavior?
- Which materials lack a viable local recovery route?
- Does organics separation materially reduce residual mass and landfill methane potential?
- Which municipality, producer-responsibility organization, processor, or donor partnership is required for scale?

## Immediate next action

Select one candidate site and complete a processor/off-taker map before purchasing bins. The first deliverable is a verified material route table, not a container purchase.
