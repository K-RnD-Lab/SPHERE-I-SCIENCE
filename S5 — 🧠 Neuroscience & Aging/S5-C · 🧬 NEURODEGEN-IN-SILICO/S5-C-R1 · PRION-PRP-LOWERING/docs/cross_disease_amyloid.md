# Phase 4 — Cross-disease amyloid reference (IN SILICO / HYPOTHESIS)

| Disease | Protein | Aggregation | ASO/siRNA in clinic | Prion-like spread |
|---------|---------|-------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Prion (CJD/FFI) | PrP (PRNP) | β-sheet prion | ION717, PrP-siRNA | Yes (defined) |
| Alzheimer | Aβ, tau | amyloid plaques | lecanemab (Ab), tau ASO (early) | debated (tau) |
| Parkinson | α-synuclein | Lewy bodies | BIIB101 (ASO, discontinued) | debated |
| ALS | SOD1, TDP-43 | aggregates | tofersen (SOD1 ASO, approved) | limited |
| Huntington | huntingtin | polyQ aggregates | tominersen (trial) | no |

**Shared platform from prion field:** intrathecal ASO/siRNA delivery, CSF biomarkers, PrP-lowering genetic validation.
