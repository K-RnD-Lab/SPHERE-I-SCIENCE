# R2 - Nutrition & Cognitive Energy

`S7-H-R2` is the nutrition-facing research line for comfortable diet design, meal structure, micronutrient awareness, supplement safety, and cognitive energy.

## Status

Active research scaffold.

This is not a treatment plan and not a supplement protocol. It is a structure for evidence review, food-comfort mapping, source discipline, and safe self-observation.

## Research question

How do nutrition patterns, meal timing, dietary quality, hydration, micronutrient status, and supplement decisions influence daily energy, focus, mood, cognitive performance, and long-range health?

## Scope

This line studies:

- comfortable baseline diet
- meal structure and satiety
- protein, fiber, hydration, and plant variety
- diet quality and cognitive state
- D3, omega-3, magnesium, and nootropic claims as evidence questions
- lab-informed and clinician-informed supplement decisions

## Initial evidence anchors

- Healthy diet guidance emphasizes vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, unsaturated fats, lower added sugar, lower salt, and lower saturated/trans fat.
- Diet quality and cognitive health are often studied through long-term patterns such as Mediterranean, DASH, and MIND-style diets.
- Supplement claims require caution because baseline status, total dose, interactions, medical history, and product quality matter.

## Starter nutrition model

This is only a research scaffold:

- build a comfortable baseline diet before optimizing details
- prioritize enough protein, fiber, colorful plants, hydration, and regular meals
- treat supplements as evidence questions, not identity or magic
- track subjective response carefully but avoid over-interpreting short-term changes
- use lab testing and clinician input for D3, iron/ferritin, B12, thyroid, or other medical questions

## Current source note

Private supplement-oriented notes should be treated as source material for evidence review only. Any health-relevant dosing, deficiency interpretation, or cognitive-stack idea requires source verification and clinician-aware safety checks before it becomes public research material.

## What belongs here

- source-backed notes about nutrition and cognition
- safe supplement evidence reviews
- meal comfort maps

## What does not belong here

- medical diagnosis
- disease treatment plans
- high-dose supplement instructions
- private lab results without redaction
- claims that a supplement or diet treats a condition
- advice to ignore clinicians, medication, contraindications, or symptoms
