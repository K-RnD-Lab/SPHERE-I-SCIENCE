# Evidence Map

## Purpose

This map defines the first evidence questions for `S7-H-R1`.

## Physical activity

### Working evidence anchor

Adults generally benefit from regular aerobic activity and muscle-strengthening activity.

Initial guideline anchors:

- WHO physical activity guidelines: 150-300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity or 75-150 minutes of vigorous activity weekly, plus muscle-strengthening activity.
- CDC adult activity overview: at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity weekly and 2 days of muscle-strengthening activity.
- American Heart Association recommendations: similar weekly aerobic targets, strength training at least 2 days per week, and less sedentary time.

## Cognitive-energy questions

- Does walking improve perceived mental clarity on the same day?
- Does running improve mood and focus, or does it create fatigue when recovery is weak?
- Does strength training correlate with better energy stability across weeks?
- What role do sleep, stress, soreness, and workload play as confounders?
- What is the minimum repeatable routine that improves energy without creating pressure?

## Movement categories

| Category | Research angle | Tracking signal |
|---|---|---|
| Walking | baseline movement, recovery, low-friction consistency | minutes, steps, energy after walk |
| Running | aerobic conditioning, mood, cardiovascular load | duration, effort, soreness, recovery |
| Strength training | muscle, metabolic health, confidence, resilience | sessions, movement type, effort |
| Mobility/recovery | soreness management and adherence support | minutes, soreness, sleep |
| Sedentary breaks | energy and focus during work/study | break frequency, focus rating |
