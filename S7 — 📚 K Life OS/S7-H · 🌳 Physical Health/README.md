# S7-H - Physical Health

A sub-lane inside `S7 - K Life OS`.

## Focus

- Physical health routines, energy patterns, body maintenance, recovery, and health-facing behaviors.
- Nutrition, meal structure, movement, walking, running, gym training, home workouts, sleep-adjacent recovery, and measurable self-care systems.
- Evidence-facing interpretation of how lifestyle inputs may influence cognitive energy, mood, focus, resilience, and long-range health.

## Current project

- [R1 - Nutrition, Movement & Cognitive Energy](./R1%20-%20Nutrition%2C%20Movement%20%26%20Cognitive%20Energy/)

## Boundary rule

Use `S7-H` when the primary object of study is the body-facing layer of life systems: food, activity, recovery, body signals, conditioning, and health routines.

Keep clinical diagnosis, treatment, disease claims, and supplement dosing decisions outside the public research layer unless they are handled with medical supervision and source discipline.

## Safety note

This lane is for research organization, evidence review, and self-tracking design. It is not medical advice, nutrition prescription, supplement prescription, or a replacement for a clinician.
